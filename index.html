    About this guide

    This guide teaches the perioperative management of cardiac implantable electronic devices (CIEDs). It follows the cardiothoracic fellowship lecture on CIEDs, the four-part review series on device interrogation for anesthesiologists (Abbott/St. Jude, Boston Scientific, Biotronik, Medtronic), and a two-sided bedside card. Each value carries its source; open Sources for the list.

    Every strip is simulated. One timing model of a pacemaker and defibrillator draws every ECG and marker channel. Its intervals, refractory periods and detection rules are typical values chosen to show each behavior; they are not any manufacturer's algorithm, and detection and charge times vary by device and programming.

    The bedside card is a draft for discussion and is not approved. Items it marks confirm are unresolved and are shown with that tag here. Where newer sources on the card differ from the 2018–2020 reviews, both are shown with their dates.

    This is an educational resource. It does not replace the device's manual, the manufacturer's technical service, the patient's CIED team or local policy. Interrogation and programming should be done by trained, credentialed practitioners.
